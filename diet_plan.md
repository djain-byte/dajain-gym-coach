# Master Jain Vegetarian Diet Blueprint: Precision Macros for Muscle & Vascularity

## 1. Scientific Macro Targets for Body Recomposition
- **Target Calories:** ~2,000 - 2,050 kcal/day (Slight 200 kcal deficit below your TDEE to burn belly/lower-back fat while building dense muscle)
- **Protein:** **145g** (~29% of calories) — *Crucial for building the dense chest, delts, and arms in Image 4*
- **Carbohydrates:** **210g** (~41% of calories) — *Fuel for high-intensity lifting and glycogen fullness*
- **Fats:** **52g** (~23% of calories) — *Controlled healthy fats for hormone health (testosterone), eliminating hidden grease*
- **Water:** 3.5 - 4.0 Litres daily (Essential for creatine uptake, digestion, and cell volumization)

## 3. The 6-Meal Daily Protocol (100% Jain Vegetarian)

### **Meal 1: Pre-Workout (6:15 AM - 6:30 AM)**
- 1 Medium Ripe Banana (~105 kcal, 27g Carbs)
- 1 Sachet (2g) Nescafe Black Coffee in 150ml warm/cold water (No sugar, no milk)
- 300ml water with 1 pinch of Himalayan Pink Rock Salt (Electrolyte pump)
*Macros: ~110 kcal | 1g Protein | 27g Carbs | 0.3g Fat*

---

### **Workout Window: 7:00 AM - 8:15 AM**
*Sip on plain water (at least 750ml throughout the session).*

---

### **Meal 2: Post-Workout Power Breakfast (8:45 AM - 9:00 AM)**
- **The Anabolic Shake:**
  - 1 Scoop ON Gold Standard 100% Whey (Double Rich Chocolate) = **24g Protein**
  - 1 Scoop (5g) Wellcore Micronised Creatine Monohydrate
  - 250ml Water (or chilled skimmed milk)
  - 10 Soaked Almonds + 2 Walnuts (eaten separately)
- **Clean Jain Breakfast Options (Pick ONE):**
  - *Option A:* 2 Moong Dal / Besan Chillas cooked with 1 tsp ghee/oil (stuffed with 30g grated paneer).
  - *Option B:* 50g Rolled Oats cooked in water, topped with a dash of cinnamon.
  - *Option C:* 1 bowl Poha or Upma cooked with Jain-friendly veggies (peas, capsicum, tomato) and only 1 tsp oil.
*Macros: ~460 kcal | 35g Protein | 42g Carbs | 14g Fat*

---

### **Meal 3: Nutrient-Dense Clean Lunch (1:00 PM - 1:30 PM)**
- **150g Low-Fat Paneer** (Raw or lightly pan-tossed in non-stick pan with jeera, turmeric, and black pepper) = **~32g Protein**
  *(If using regular full-fat paneer, take 100g and reduce fats elsewhere)*
- **2 Medium Phulkas / Rotis** (Made from whole wheat or multigrain, no butter/ghee spread) = **~140 kcal, 30g Carbs, 5g Protein**
- **1 Medium Bowl Thick Dal** (Yellow Moong / Chana Dal / Toor Dal) = **~8g Protein**
- **1 Bowl Jain Sabji** (Bhindi, Lauki, Turai, Capsicum, Parwal, or Cabbage cooked in minimal oil)
- **Fresh Salad:** Cucumber, tomato, lemon squeeze, pinch of black salt.
*Macros: ~540 kcal | 45g Protein | 55g Carbs | 12g Fat*

---

### **Meal 4: Late Afternoon Sustained Energy (4:30 PM - 5:00 PM)**
- 1 Scoop ON Gold Standard Whey Protein (shaken in 200ml cold water) = **24g Protein**
- 1 Whole Fresh Fruit: Apple, Pear, Guava, or 150g Papaya (High fiber, micronutrients)
*Macros: ~210 kcal | 25g Protein | 25g Carbs | 1.5g Fat*

---

### **Meal 5: Muscle-Repair Clean Dinner (7:30 PM - 8:00 PM)**
*Finish dinner by 8:00 PM to give your digestive system 3 hours before sleep.*
- **Protein Source (Pick ONE):**
  - *Option A (Top choice):* **40g Soya Chunks (Nutrela)** boiled and cooked in tomato-jeera Jain gravy = **~21g Protein, 130 kcal, 0.5g Fat**. *(Note: Soya chunks are 100% vegetarian plant protein from defatted soybeans).*
  - *Option B:* 2 Moong Dal Chillas stuffed with 50g Low-Fat Paneer = **~20g Protein**.
  - *Option C:* 1 Bowl thick Rajma or Black Chana curry + 100g Low-Fat Curd / Hung Dahi = **~18g Protein**.
- **Carbs & Fiber:**
  - 1 to 2 Thin Phulkas OR 1 small cup Brown/White Rice
  - 1 Bowl Green Veggie Sabji
*Macros: ~450 kcal | 28g Protein | 52g Carbs | 9g Fat*

---

### **Meal 6: Bedtime Recovery (Optional / Before 10:30 PM)**
- 1 Cup Warm Skimmed Milk with a pinch of Haldi (Turmeric) and black pepper (anti-inflammatory, aids sleep).
*Macros: ~90 kcal | 8g Protein | 10g Carbs | 1g Fat*
